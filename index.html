02 / Research

Questions worth investigating.

My work explores the reliability of machine learning where errors can have real consequences.

Manuscript completed2026 · Preparing for submission

Directional Constraints for Patient-Specific Alert Thresholds in Early Sepsis Monitoring: An Empirical Bayes Analysis

Collaborative research with Dr. Md Tuhin Sheikh

Investigated why patient-specific alert thresholds can reduce false alarms yet suppress early sepsis warnings. Evaluated directionally constrained Empirical Bayes adaptation using 40,336 ICU patient records, comparing sensitivity and clinical utility across multiple adaptation histories.

The constrained approach mitigated losses associated with unrestricted personalization; superiority over the global threshold was not conclusively established.

Undergraduate thesis2025–2026

JASAL: A Remote Patient Health Monitoring System Driven by Federated Learning

BRAC University · Undergraduate thesis

Developed a privacy-aware federated anomaly detection system using 32,796 physiological records across 10 non-IID client partitions. Explored adaptive patient profiles, differential privacy, secure computation, and INT8 quantization.
